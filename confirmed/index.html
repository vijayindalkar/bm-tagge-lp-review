What happens at your visit

Your consultation is one 90-minute visit, in place of the usual three. Here is how it goes:

  1. Dr. Tagge examines your whole forefoot and talks through what is going on.
  2. He takes your x-rays and a biomechanical scan on-site, reviews your history, and explains what he sees in plain language.
  3. You get a direct answer on whether your foot can be corrected.
  4. If you are a candidate, you and Dr. Tagge design the correction and the recovery together.

You leave with a real answer and a plan.

What to bring

  • Any prior imaging you already have (optional; your x-rays and a scan are done at the visit).
  • A short history of what you have tried.
  • Your questions. Nothing is too small.
  • Your spouse, or whoever helps you decide, if you would like.
