THE PRISM™ APPROACH

Your Recovery Belongs in the Plan From the Start

You want to address your bunion without putting the rest of your life on hold. That matters to Dr. Tagge, too.

He built PRISM™ to plan the correction and the recovery together, with your foot, your health, and your daily life in mind.

  1. Before

    Understand What Your Foot Needs

    Dr. Tagge looks at your whole forefoot, your health history, and the activities you want back. Recovery needs are considered before surgery.

  2. During

    Make the Correction Through Tiny Incisions

    The procedure is chosen for your foot, with the aim of limiting needless surgical disruption. Permanent screws and plates aren’t routinely used.

  3. After

    Guide Your Return to Activity

    Your plan covers how to protect the correction, move safely, and build activity back up. Recovery support is planned around your foot and your goals.

If a hammertoe or another forefoot issue also needs attention, Dr. Tagge will assess whether it can be addressed in the same procedure and recovery.
